WEEK 07 · Menu and budget
合計と残金を計算Calculate the total and money left
totalは0から始め、選んだ商品の値段を足す。残金は予算−合計。Start total at zero and add each selected price. Money left is budget minus total.
Python
budget = 1000
total = 0
for item in order:
    total += item["price"]
print("Total:", total, "yen")
print("Money left:", budget - total, "yen")
Total: 470 yen
Money left: 530 yen

メニューをCSVから読むRead the menu from CSV
csv.DictReaderは各行を辞書として読む。priceは文字列なのでintで数値にする。CSVとNotebookは同じフォルダへ。csv.DictReader reads each row as a dictionary. Convert price from text to a number with int. Keep the CSV beside your notebook.
Python
import csv

with open("student-menu.csv") as file:
    menu = list(csv.DictReader(file))
for item in menu:
    item["price"] = int(item["price"])
print(len(menu), "items")
