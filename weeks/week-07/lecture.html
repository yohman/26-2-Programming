選んだプランの合計Check your plan’s total
例はおにぎり＋サンドイッチ。金額と時間をforで足し、予算と時間の両方を確認。表示された候補を全部選ぶ必要はない。This example selects onigiri and a sandwich. Add their cost and time with for, then check both limits. You choose which options go into your plan.
Python
budget = 1000
max_minutes = 45
plan = [menu[0], menu[1]]
total_price = 0
total_minutes = 0
for item in plan:
    total_price += item["price"]
    total_minutes += item["minutes"]
print("Cost:", total_price, "yen")
print("Money left:", budget - total_price, "yen")
print("Time:", total_minutes, "minutes")
print("Within budget:", total_price <= budget)
print("Within time:", total_minutes <= max_minutes)
